Room Kit

A drum kit you can place in your room and actually hit. Put the headset on, pick a mode, then move the pieces where you want them.

Swing
Hit a drum with the stick. Harder swing, louder hit.
Trigger
Kick drum
Grip
Hi-hat pedal — hold to close the hat
A or X
Switch between playing and moving pieces
B or Y
Bring the whole kit back in front of you

No headset handy? Play from the keyboard: K kick, S snare, H hat, G open hat, 1 2 3 toms, C crash, R ride. Drag to look around.

WebXR needs a secure origin. Localhost works for desktop testing, but the headset needs the page over HTTPS.
